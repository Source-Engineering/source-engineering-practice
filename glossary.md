# Practitioner Glossary

> Practitioner-friendly terms mapped to the discipline's canonical terms.

The discipline's canonical terms are in `Source-Engineering/source-engineering-foundations/terminology.md`. This glossary maps practitioner-friendly terms to the canonical terms; the canonical terms are normative.

## A

**Audit** -> **Audit trail.** The persistent record of who saw what response, when, with what evidence, and what they did with it. Part of Layer 4 of the discipline's four-layer stack.

**Audit history** -> **Audit trail (per-decision).** The append-only log of changes to a single DecisionRecord.

**Appeal** -> **Feedback surface (type: appeal).** A COTS's `validation.feedback_surfaces` entry that lets the user file an appeal against a decision.

## B

**Blueprint** -> **Architecture blueprint.** A versioned architectural specification that floors one or more layers of the stack.

**Bundle** -> **COTS (Chain of Orchestrable and Traceable Sources).** The structured, machine-readable bundle that every Source Engineering response carries.

## C

**Chain of evidence** -> **Evidence chain.** The chain that links a decision back to its supporting and contradicting evidence.

**COTS** -> **COTS (Chain of Orchestrable and Traceable Sources).** The discipline's headline artefact. Five parts: Inputs, Context, Decisions, Evidence, Validation.

**Compatibility** -> **Backwards compatibility.** The discipline's guarantee that a v1.x COTS producer is acceptable to a v1 COTS consumer.

**Conformance** -> **Conformance.** The property of an implementation that satisfies the discipline's specifications. Verified by the conformance suite.

**Counter-evidence** -> **Counter-evidence.** The subset of evidence items that contradicts a decision or contradicts a claim the decision relies on. Mandatory.

## D

**Decision** -> **DecisionRecord.** The persistent artefact that captures a decision. Part of the COTS.

**Decision basis** -> **DecisionBasis.** The subset of the COTS that links a decision to its supporting evidence, counterevidence, context, constraints, and known gaps.

**Domain** -> **High-stakes domain.** A vertical where a confidently wrong decision carries a cost the user is not willing to absorb through latency.

## E

**Evidence** -> **Evidence chain + EvidenceItem.** The chain that links a decision back to its sources, and the items in the chain.

**Explanation** -> **DecisionBasis + Evidence chain + Context.** The discipline's surface for explaining a decision. The explanation is auditable.

## F

**Feedback surface** -> **Validation surface.** An endpoint in the COTS's `validation.feedback_surfaces` that lets the user interact with the decision. Three types: appeal, rerun, correction.

**Five pillars** -> **The five pillars (P1-P5).** Data Provenance, Contextual Signature Mapping, Chain of Evidence, Explainability Anchoring, Human Validation Points. The 2025 essay's intellectual shape.

**Four layers** -> **The four-layer stack.** Typed Model, Runtime Validation, Business Guardrails, Audit Trail. The 2026 essay's architectural shape.

## H

**Hesitation factor** -> **Hesitation factor.** The reluctance to act on a model's answer because the user cannot verify its reasoning in time. The discipline exists to close this.

## J

**Judgment gap** -> **Judgment gap.** The gap between a structurally valid answer and the right answer. The discipline's second-order problem.

## L

**Layer** -> **Layer.** One of the four named layers of the discipline's stack: Typed Model, Runtime Validation, Business Guardrails, Audit Trail.

**Log** -> **Audit trail (operator-facing).** A COTS's audit trail is more than a log; a log is operator-facing, an audit trail is user-facing.

## M

**Manifesto** -> **Manifesto.** The discipline's canonical home, in `Source-Engineering/source-engineering-foundations/docs/00-manifesto.md`.

**Model card** -> **Model card.** A COTS-aligned model card. The model card is generated by the `model-card-gen` tool in `Source-Engineering/source-engineering-tools/`.

## P

**Pillar** -> **Pillar.** One of the five named claims introduced in the 2025 essay. The pillars remain the language of the foundations; the layers are the language of the architecture.

**Provenance** -> **Provenance.** The chain that links an evidence item back to its source.

## R

**Reasoning trace** -> **Trace + COTS.** A COTS is more than a reasoning trace; a trace is the model's internal log, a COTS is the discipline's auditable response.

**Re-run** -> **Feedback surface (type: rerun).** A COTS's `validation.feedback_surfaces` entry that re-runs the decision with the same evidence under a different model or version.

## S

**Source** -> **SourceArtifact.** A discrete piece of content that can be cited as an input to a decision.

**Spec** -> **Specification.** A normative technical requirement, in `Source-Engineering/source-engineering-specifications/`.

**Stack** -> **Four-layer stack.** The composition of the discipline into four named layers.

## T

**Trace** -> **Trace + COTS.** A COTS is the discipline's trace; the operator-facing trace is the model's internal log.

**Typed question** -> **Typed question (per a registered decision-type).** A question that declares its answer shape in advance.

## V

**Validation** -> **Validation.** The subset of a COTS that captures the feedback surfaces exposed back to the user. Not the same as the runtime-validation layer (Layer 2).

**Version** -> **Version.** A specific state of a source artifact, addressed via content hash.
